The car

Seller's summary

This is the Turbo: the 4.8-liter twin-turbocharged V8 with 500 hp, an 8-speed Tiptronic S and all-wheel drive. Porsche quoted 0 to 60 mph in 4.4 seconds when it was new. It was built in February 2013 and delivered in St. Louis with a $130,185 sticker.

The color combination is Jet Black Metallic over the two-tone Espresso and Cognac natural leather interior, with cognac seat belts and the walnut interior package. Options include 21-inch 911 Turbo II wheels, the panorama roof, Burmester surround sound, 14-way memory seats and Premium Package Plus. It also has adaptive cruise control, shown working in the videos.

It spent its first twelve years in the St. Louis area, where Carfax shows regular dealer service, most of it at Porsche St. Louis. It sold on Bring a Trailer in 2025, spent about nine months in Texas, and has been in Aiken since August 2026. The seller reports it presents much as it did at that auction and is dry underneath. There are 18 photos of the underside in the gallery so you can judge for yourself.

One accident appears on the Carfax, from January 2014. It was repaired, and the windshield has since been replaced. Both are covered under History, and known flaws are under Condition.
